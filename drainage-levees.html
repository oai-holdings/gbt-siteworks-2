Drainage & leveesGroesbeck & Central Texas

Getting water to go where it should, and keeping it away from where it shouldn't.

Request an estimate Call 254-279-0808

Water always wins, unless the ground is shaped right.

Flooded barns, washed-out driveways, standing water in the yard, pasture that stays boggy for weeks. Water problems get worse every season they're left alone.

We dig ditches, build levees and berms, set culverts and reshape ground so water moves off your property the right way.

What's included

  • DitchingNew ditches and cleaning out old ones.
  • Levees & bermsHolding water back or steering it away.
  • CulvertsCrossings for driveways and roads.
  • SwalesGentle channels that carry runoff.
  • Yard & house drainageMoving water away from foundations.
  • Property shapingRegrading to fix how the land drains.

How the job goes

  1. Describe the problemWhere water sits, and when it shows up.
  2. Look after rainSeeing where water actually goes.
  3. Plan & estimateThe fix and how it ties into the rest of the land.
  4. BuildDitches, levees, culverts and grading.
  5. Check itMake sure water moves the way it should.

What affects the price

Every property is different, so we price after we've seen it. These are the things that move the number.

  • Scale of the problemOne low spot versus a whole field.
  • Dirt movedLevees and berms take a lot of material.
  • CulvertsPipe size and number of crossings.
  • Ground conditionsWet sites slow the work.
  • PermitsWork near creeks or floodplains may need approval.

Your water is your neighbor's water too

Changing how water leaves your property can affect the people downstream, and work near creeks, floodplains or larger dams may need permits. Texas also has rules about blocking or redirecting natural water flow.

We'll help you find a fix that solves your problem without creating one next door.
