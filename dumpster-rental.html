Dumpster rentalGroesbeck & Central Texas

Roll-off dumpsters dropped off where you need them and hauled off when you're done. Cleanouts, demo, roofing and build sites.

Request an estimate Call 254-279-0808

Somewhere to put it all.

Cleaning out an old house, tearing off a roof, clearing out a barn or running a build site, the job goes faster when the trash has somewhere to go.

We drop a roll-off where you need it, you fill it, and we haul it off. Pairing it with a demo or clearing job? We'll time the dumpster to the work.

What's included

  • Home cleanoutsEstates, moves, garages and storage.
  • Demolition debrisTear-outs, sheds and small structures.
  • Roofing jobsShingle tear-offs.
  • Construction sitesOngoing debris on a build.
  • Yard & brushLimbs and yard waste.
  • Property cleanupBarns, lots and rental turnovers.

How the job goes

  1. CallTell us the job, the dates and where it's going.
  2. Pick a sizeWe'll help you choose the right one.
  3. Drop offSet where you want it, on a spot that can take the weight.
  4. Fill itLoad it up within the rules.
  5. Haul offWe pick it up and handle disposal.

What affects the price

Every property is different, so we price after we've seen it. These are the things that move the number.

  • Dumpster sizeBigger cans cost more but save extra trips.
  • Rental lengthHow many days you keep it.
  • WeightHeavy loads like concrete or dirt weigh out fast.
  • DistanceHow far the drop-off is from us.
  • MaterialSome materials cost more to dispose of.

What can't go in

Most dumpsters can't take hazardous materials. That usually means paint, chemicals, oil, batteries, tires, propane tanks and appliances that still hold refrigerant. Ask before you toss anything you're unsure about.

Pick the drop spot ahead of time, too. A loaded roll-off is heavy and can crack a thin driveway, so firm, level ground is best. Boards under the wheels help.
