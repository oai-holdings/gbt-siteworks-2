DemolitionGroesbeck & Central Texas

Old houses, mobile homes, barns and outbuildings torn down, hauled off and the ground left ready to use again.

Request an estimate Call 254-279-0808

Clear the old so you can build the new.

A falling-down house, an old mobile home, a barn that's past saving. Sometimes the first job on a property is getting rid of what's already there.

We take it down, handle the debris, and restore the site so it's ready for whatever comes next. That's often a new pad, which we can build too.

What's included

  • House demolitionOlder homes and structures taken down safely.
  • Mobile home removalOld units torn down and removed.
  • Barns & outbuildingsSheds, barns, pens and old structures.
  • Foundation removalSlabs and piers broken out and removed.
  • Debris cleanupHauling off and disposal.
  • Site restorationGround filled, graded and ready to use.

How the job goes

  1. Tell us what's coming downPhotos help a lot.
  2. Look it overSize, materials, access and what's around it.
  3. EstimateDemo, hauling and restoration in one price.
  4. Take it downUtilities confirmed off before work starts.
  5. RestoreDebris gone, ground graded and ready.

What affects the price

Every property is different, so we price after we've seen it. These are the things that move the number.

  • Size & constructionWood, metal, block and slab all differ.
  • Hauling & disposalDistance and the amount of debris.
  • FoundationWhether slabs or piers come out too.
  • AccessRoom to work around the structure.
  • Site restorationFill and grading after the debris is gone.

Before the walls come down

Utilities have to be disconnected before demolition, so call the electric co-op, water supply and propane provider ahead of time. Some buildings, especially commercial ones, also need an asbestos inspection before they can be demolished.

We'll tell you up front what your project needs, so nothing holds up the job once the equipment is on site.
