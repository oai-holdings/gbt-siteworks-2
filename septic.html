Septic systemsGroesbeck & Central Texas

New and replacement septic systems, installed by a licensed installer, with the clearing, pad and dirt work handled by the same crew.

Request an estimate Call 254-279-0808

Out here, the septic is part of the site.

Most rural homesites in Central Texas run on septic, and it has to be planned alongside the pad, the driveway and the drainage. Put the house in the wrong spot and you can end up with nowhere legal to put the system.

We plan the septic with the rest of the site from day one. Installation is done by a TCEQ-licensed installer, and our crew handles the clearing, access and grading around it, so it's one job instead of three.

What's included

  • New system installsFor new homes and manufactured homes.
  • System replacementsWhen an old system fails or a home is replaced.
  • Permit coordinationWorking through the county or TCEQ permit process.
  • Site evaluationSoil and site evaluation set up before design.
  • Clearing & accessThe system area cleared and reachable for equipment.
  • Final gradeGround restored and shaped to drain away from the system.

How the job goes

  1. Call usWhere the property is and what's going on it.
  2. Site evaluationSoil and site checked to decide what system will work.
  3. Design & permitSystem designed and permitted before any digging.
  4. InstallInstalled by the licensed installer, then inspected.
  5. FinishFinal grade and cleanup around the system.

What affects the price

Every property is different, so we price after we've seen it. These are the things that move the number.

  • System typeSoil and lot size decide which system the site needs.
  • Soil conditionsHeavy clay and high water tables change the design.
  • Home sizeBedrooms and water use set the system size.
  • Site workClearing, access and grading around the system.
  • Replacement vs. newRemoving an old system adds work.

Plan the septic before the pad

In Texas, a septic system needs a site evaluation, a design and a permit before installation, and it gets inspected before it's covered. Setbacks from wells, property lines, water and the house decide where it can go.

That's why the septic should be planned before the pad goes in. We lay out the house, the drive and the system together so they all fit, and nothing has to be moved later.
